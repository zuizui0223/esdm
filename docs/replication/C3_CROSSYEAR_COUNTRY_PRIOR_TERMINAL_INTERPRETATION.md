## Why the original experiment cannot be declared successful or rescued

The frozen eligible exposure and physical-camera definition treats a device as a repeatable station *within a fixed survey array*. A device moving between arrays breaks the representation and may introduce correlated detections between locations and a false notion of camera-level independence. Treating each deployment as a new independent camera, or constructing a location crosswalk from placename/longitude/latitude, could be scientifically reasonable **in a prospectively different study**, but would change this experiment's exact frozen observational unit and screening support. The C3 rules expressly forbid such result-dependent repair.

We do not switch to `(year, project_id, deployment_id)` or a position-based key, relax the per-array identity check, alter 20-day exposure, adjust the 2021 roster, split on 2022 outcomes, tune Brier-gain thresholds, or repeat the same opening. We preserve the stopped one-shot as the **final C3 ecological endpoint**. No Brier score, difference between global and country priors, true country species-pool effect, or E5 detection pass has been established.

## Constructive next scientific direction

This suggests a **general observational-design distinction** for camera ecology: physical *device* identity, stable sampling *station* identity, deployment/operational *interval* identity, and independent reference-*passage* identity are distinct keys. A valid future study must document an independently audited device-to-station-to-deployment crosswalk **before accessing biological outcomes** and place unknown crosswalks into nonestimable support. This is stronger than choosing a new statistical model on the same source. In terms of the pre-existing [minimal identifiability audit](POOL_STATE_DETECTION_IDENTIFICATION_AUDIT.md), better station crosswalks establish the validity of repeated-observation units but do not themselves identify regional species pool vs local occupancy or availability vs capture.

All original E4/E5/E7, Safari14, CamTrapAsia C1 and NEON C2 outcomes stay frozen. **E5 detection G4 remains unqualified (0/17 standard candidates)**. This is a source-schema/observational-grain stop, **not** an ecological hypothesis falsification or an empirical success.
