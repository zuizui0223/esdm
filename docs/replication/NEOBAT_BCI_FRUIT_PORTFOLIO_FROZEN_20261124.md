## Non-adjustable analyses
1. Validate actual CSV fields `year,sp4,family,genus,species,abscised_seeds,viable_seeds,total_seeds,proportion_abscised,sum_parts`; retain species-year rows (dedup by `sp4,year` and reject contradictory duplicates). Require nonnegative finite counts and `abscised_seeds+viable_seeds ≈ total_seeds` (exact within 1% or absolute 2 due to published rounding). Document malformed/missing fields, years, source version, SHA-256 and all 201 species labels.
2. Exact normalized `genus + species` binomial overlap to the *frozen* 106-plant roster and original NeoBat fruit-bat links. Preserve all 106 as matched/not matched without treating no match as biological absence; no synonym guessing.
3. For each source candidate bat, use its locally present **source-listed** plants with >=3 species and >=10 *jointly observed calendar years*. In original Dryad data, omitted species-years remain **missing**, not zeros. For every species, transform yearly `log1p(viable_seeds)` and standardize by the series mean of transformed values; average transformed relative series equally across species. Record observed annual between-year coefficient of variation (CV).
4. Temporal synchrony null: circularly shift each species' standardized annual sequence independently (499 replicates; seed 20261124), while preserving within-series values and temporal serial structure. A ratio `observed_CV / median(null_CV)` below 1 describes comparatively compensatory patterns; above 1 comparatively synchronous patterns. Report all bats and ratios, including null/ineligible outcomes; **do not** call ratios a hypothesis-test p-value, and no single-species weighting fitted to outcomes.
5. Secondary descriptive: matched-species annual premature fraction `sum(abscised)/sum(total)` where denominator is positive; species/year coverage, and overlap for each candidate bat. Do not treat seed counts as grams of edible fruit. No species/site detection assumption, no causal interpretation of guild or model superiority.

## Strong biological limitations
* One island (BCI) with shared seed traps, so the candidate bat portfolios are not independent local or geographic replications. Original NeoBat links are observed across many sites and **not confirmed as BCI bat feeding links**; a candidate portfolio is not a realized bat diet.
* Published viable/abscised seeds are an annual reproductive-output proxy, not directly measured ripe fruit availability or bat intake. Species have different seeds-per-fruit factors, trait and guild composition, and unobserved years; complete-case annual alignment can severely shrink effective sample size.
* No contemporaneous bat abundance/activity/outcome is used in this pilot. No original M3–M1/N1 bat predictive gain or eSDM superiority claim; if source download is blocked, stop transparently rather than fabricate or substitute.
* All analyses strictly exploratory. Report weak, null and negative patterns, not just favorable bat taxa. Preserve only aggregated metadata, species names, years and anonymized bat portfolio results; no raw trap coordinates or individual animal locations. Source files remain ephemeral, with file SHA-256 and provenance in outputs.
