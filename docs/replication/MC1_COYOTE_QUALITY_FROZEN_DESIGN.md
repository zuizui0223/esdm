## Falsifiable primary question

At **physical camera sites wholly excluded from model fitting**, do per-photo observable image attributes (`blur`, `propbodyvis`, `In_color`) add predictive information about the **label** `Mange_signs_present` beyond season and calendar year alone? This is a test of annotation predictiveness, **not** proof that image quality causes label bias, that coyote mange prevalence changes, or that camera detection conditional on animal passage is identified. Image-quality association may reflect genuine visible disease severity, disease behaviour, or sampler selection.

1. Parse only the published CSV. Enforce exact unique image `new_file_name`, nonempty `site`, calendar `Year` finite integer, season in Spring/Summer/Fall/Winter (case-insensitive), target `Mange_signs_present` exactly one of `0,1,true,false,TRUE,FALSE`, `In_color` 0/1/Boolean or NA, nonnegative finite blur when observed, body proportion `0<=propbodyvis<=1` when observed. Treat blank and `NA` as missing image attributes, imputed from **training-only** medians; no row or image is excluded on outcome direction. Target/source/site/schema errors are terminal. Do not read file-name/individual-animal information into predictions.
2. Split by **site** using SHA256 first byte of `MC1-20261119|site` (<51 → validation; otherwise training). All rows at a physical site share a partition, and no site-level ecological predictor is used. Train ≥60 sites / ≥1500 images and holdout ≥12 sites / ≥300 images, and ≥20 positive and ≥20 negative labels in *each* split; otherwise `INSUFFICIENT_SUPPORT`, with zero refitting or changing seeds.
3. Baseline `M0`: calendar year (training-centred, SD-standardized; zero-variance maps to zero) plus one-hot seasons (Spring is reference). Full `M1`: baseline plus log1p(blur) and visible-body fraction (each training-centred/SD-scaled with median imputation), photo color flag and two missing-attribute indicators (blur-missing/body-missing). No site ID, exposure outcome or date in features. Fit both via `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(C=1.0, penalty='l2', solver='lbfgs', max_iter=1000, tol=1e-8, class_weight=None, random_state=20261119)` with intercept. No tuning on validation.
4. On the *unseen-site* image-level response, calculate Brier mean squared error and log-loss for M0/M1. Primary `Brier_gain = Brier_M0 - Brier_M1`; report sign, both errors and also log-loss gain. A descriptive directional **positive** result requires Brier gain ≥+0.005, log-loss gain >0 and the lower bound of a 1,000-replicate site-cluster bootstrap of image-weighted Brier gain >0; **negative** requires gain ≤−0.005 and bootstrap upper <0; otherwise inconclusive. Use deterministic Python seed 20261119, fixed 2.5/97.5 percentiles with linear interpolation. Site bootstrap is not a confidence interval corrected for spatial autocorrelation or research-area extrapolation.
5. Export aggregate support, label prevalence, signed metrics, bootstrap limits, source blob/byte hashes and explicit inference limits. Do NOT output image filenames, site codes, GPS, individual labels, model coefficients for named species or any raw records. No p-values from per-image pseudoreplication.
