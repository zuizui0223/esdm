## Scientific question

Does the apparent success of GBIF source-derived plant SDMs come from the number of *other plants reported in a cell* rather than abiotic niche transfer, and is the phenomenon limited to three Colombian examples or repeated across the frozen 106 plants and 17 country holdouts? The 3-Colombia test already showed high source-intensity AUC; extrapolation is **not** justified until non-Colombia regions and the entire pre-fixed species pool are examined without outcome-based selection.

## Frozen model/evaluation

For every plant×country:
- The same `datasetKey`-restricted observed focal cells and other-plant pseudo-background construction as the completed source-matched Colombia 3-plant pilot. Background cell cannot have focal recorded in *any* source. Both observed positives and background cells must contain >=1 recorded **other** plant among original 255 GBIF plant cohort at that 25-km location. Shared `datasetKey` is only a publication/data collection group, not a common field survey.
- Test uses only withheld country's 25-km EPSG:6933 grid cells. Training uses the other 28 source countries, **discarding every cell present in any record from test country** to prevent crossborder cell reuse. Check disjointness before fitting. Cell centroid sampled against strictly pinned BIO1, BIO12, and elevation. Missing or nodata cells excluded from each class, count separately.
- StandardScaler fitted to training only; `LogisticRegression(C=1.0,class_weight='balanced',max_iter=400,solver='lbfgs',random_state=20261119)`. Identical train/test rows for three models: environmental three variables alone, `log1p(n other recorded plant species at this cell)` alone, and four predictors combined. No model/hyperparameter selection or censoring by heldout AUC.
- Quantity stop if, *after raster no-data*, any training class has <20 distinct within-country observed source cells or any heldout class has <5. **Never** relax those conditions. No zero-is-absence substitution, no fitted model for stopped pairs.
- Primary **process** contrast for each eligible fold `Δ_AUC = AUC_{environment+intensity} - AUC_{intensity-only}`. Secondary `AUC_{environment-only}`, `AUC_{intensity-only}` and **exact source-intensity-matched conditional AUC and matched-pair coverage** (conditional AUC from combined model only). Conditional AUC computed using the fixed, not tuned, counts of other observed taxa in both test classes; coverage can be tiny, keep null on zero comparable pairs.
- A fully independent/source-unopened confirmation is NOT claimed: other-plant recording intensity is computed from observations including heldout country, and thus may be a test-site/source proxy rather than deployable prediction. A high AUC on this variable is evidence of **source-recording discrimination**, not ecological plant-suitability performance or mapped plant biomass.
- Primary descriptive synthesis: all 1,802 status counts; eligible fold count and per-country counts; distribution of `Δ_AUC` and effort-only AUC; **equal-country** average of within-country mean deltas (countries with >=1 eligible fold), fraction of eligible folds with `Δ_AUC>0`, fraction with `AUC_{intensity-only}>AUC_{environment-only}`. Also country and species aggregates, number of countries with positive/negative mean delta and leave-one-country-out averages. These are **descriptive source-pattern tests** with shared training records, not independent country populations or causal effects. Do not derive significance from pooling cell-level pairs as independent.
- Reproduce original fixed Colombia 3-sentinel source-matched pilot source-cell denominators and all three AUCs within an absolute 1e-10 tolerance. If any differ, **STOP all source claims**. The 106-fold all-country pilot may take substantial CPU; hard STOP if runtime, source hashes, schema or prerequisites fail rather than silently fit a subset and declare completion.
- Export **only aggregate taxon×country AUCs, source-cell denominators and STOPs**; no original coordinates, GBIF IDs, sample-level scores, per-cell records, raster arrays, dataset keys, or source-observation maps may leave memory.

## Conclusion ceiling

This is an **exploratory empirical source-reporting bias generalization audit**. Irrespective of effect signs, it neither supplies the 106×17 crossfitted per-cell plant relative suitability surfaces required by bat M3 nor tests M3−M1/N1. Even successful plant rank prediction would not establish feeding/resource mechanisms. Continue ecology only after independently defensible plant and sampling-bias predictors, complete target-group background and 17-country environmental raster coverage are qualified.
