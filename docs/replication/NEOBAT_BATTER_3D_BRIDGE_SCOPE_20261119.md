# NeoBat 2D -> batter 3D transfer bridge — scope boundary (2026-11-19)

This is a model-admission and data-compatibility note, not a positive
trophic mechanism result.

## Existing independent movement evidence

The batter project (https://github.com/zuizui0223/batter) has six
high-resolution bat flight-height panels representing four taxa:
*Tadarida teniotis*, *Eidolon helvum*, *Hypsignathus monstrosus* and
*Phyllostomus hastatus* (three P. hastatus panels: 2016, 2022, 2023).
Five non-Tadarida panels support repeatable centered vertical
distribution shape. The maintained claim is individuality in
probability distributions, not determined feeding behavior or stable routes.

## What can actually join NeoBat?

The NeoBat source has 27 raw records of Interaction=Frugivory for
*Phyllostomus hastatus* from 9 reference codes; 16 records have
two-token binomial-looking plant species names from 7 references.
All 27 are classified TrophicGuild=Omnivore. Therefore P. hastatus
is NOT in the 26-species primary Frugivore-only network cohort,
even though it is a potential *secondary bridge case*.

- No existing batter telemetry panel from the 26 primary NeoBat
  frugivore candidate species was identified.
- Eidolon and Hypsignathus are African, outside NeoBat Neotropical
  plant partner coverage; importing NeoBat links for them is invalid.
- Tadarida's insect diet is not represented by NeoBat fruit links.
- P. hastatus needs a mixed-resource model, not a single
  frugivory score inferred as complete food availability.

## Valid secondary tests

1. Use predeclared and provenance-screened P. hastatus plant links
   as a potential fruit-partner predictor only, while clearly
   labelling omission of animal prey and other food.
2. Predict coarse encounter opportunities from plant geography,
   forest/landscape structure and independently resolved plant
   life forms, NOT exact fruiting height.
3. Test held-out individual/session height distributions conditional
   on horizontal space with an explicit altitude observation model,
   as a future transfer evaluation separate from the phase-1 2D
   trophic prediction.
4. Do not claim diet choice, realized fruit encounter, causal
   feeding-state effects, or a species-wide vertical SDM without
   independent state/behavior and detection evidence.

## Primary-to-3D bridge boundary

The first primary 2D question may be answerable for candidate
frugivorous Neotropical bats using GBIF, but those are different
taxa from the tracked batter panels. A genuine 3D trophic test needs
either (a) independently accessible vertical observations for one or
more primary 26 species or (b) a separate African bat-plant interaction
dataset and joint movement/trait calibration. Do not claim a direct
empirical 2D-to-3D fit until the taxonomic overlap is established.
