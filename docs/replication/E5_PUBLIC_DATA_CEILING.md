# E5 public-data ceiling

Status: **FROZEN — candidate search closed**

E5 screened 15 candidates response-blind and qualified 0. No focal biological response
was opened for candidate selection.

The repeated failure is structural, not a shortage of records. The scarce property is
the joint availability of:

1. a detection channel identifiable separately from ecological activity; and
2. geographic overlap of that detection/source/protocol factor.

The strongest recent candidate, Henrich10, passed the public-methods G3 design check and
had ample temporal and physical replication. Its one-shot OSF manifest nevertheless
stopped at G4: effort files and CTDS animal-distance outputs were public, but the
independent ranging/reference-calibration material described by the study was not
reproducibly exposed in the public package.

This closes generic E5 candidate discovery. The registry stays at **15 screened / 0
qualified**. Kays41 remains an unresolved reference, not an active repair target and not
a pass.

## Reopening rule

E5 may reopen only if a candidate surfaces independently of E5 outcome values and public
methods/schema/metadata already show, before focal response opening:

- a separately identifiable detection path;
- overlap of that path across at least two geographic regimes;
- plausible capacity for the frozen physical-location and six-month temporal gates.

Previously stopped candidates may not be repaired using focal response, repeated
authorization, or post-hoc schema exploration.

Until such a candidate appears, E4 remains the terminal empirical endpoint. A future
route that relaxes deployment-linked detection identification or changes the estimand
must be separately named rather than presented as an E5 rescue.

The ceiling is a statement about currently screened public-data support under the frozen
contract. It is **not** evidence that activity is irrelevant, detection is unimportant,
CTDS is invalid, or no qualifying dataset exists anywhere.
