# S14-FM: Are observed mammal reserve turnovers unusual under fixed camera/species incidence margins?

**A new post-result conditional-incidence sensitivity, frozen on 2026-11-14 before the S14-FM camera-level response is opened.** Original Safari14 β-diversity source outcome and its positive *descriptive* replacement-dominance result, plus the weakness shown by the retrospective **species-list-only** random null (PR #231), are known. This is **not** a new provider, a G4 detection calibration, a species trait test, an environmental-causal inference or a repeat of the earlier Safari14 primary run. Do not change these rules after results.

## The more defensible (but still limited) question

**After conditioning jointly on (i) the number of recorded mammal labels per eligible physical camera and (ii) each mammal label's number of cameras across a fixed park partition, is the observed median *Simpson replacement dissimilarity* between parks greater than the value under exchangeable assignments of those records among cameras?**

This is a different, stricter question than "is β_sim greater than β_sne?" — a criterion that can hold in entirely random species-list assignments even when there is no park-specific ecological sorting. The new conditional null tests the **recorded cross-park species-sorting signal** under camera-level fixed row/column margins, not true ecological occupancy.

## Exact fixed-margins randomization

**Randomize each 7- or 5-park partition *separately***, with its fixed set of eligible source camera locations. Build a binary camera×taxon incidence table from the **original taxon label presence at each camera**, not an image-count taxon frequency. The randomization treats cameras exchangeably across parks **within a partition**. In every simulated matrix it preserves **exactly**:

1. Each camera's number of observed wild mammal labels (row sum);
2. Each label's total number of eligible physical cameras across that partition (column sum);
3. The number and identities of eligible camera locations per project, the 7/5 park split and the 10-camera sampling plan.

Swap assignments by the **curveball/trade algorithm**: for two randomly selected camera rows, retain their intersecting labels, randomly reallocate the labels exclusive to one or the other without replacement while preserving both exclusive-set sizes. The resulting row/column margins are invariant by construction, including zero-richness camera rows. Never permute raw images or camera effort across sensor protocols.

**One predeclared finite sensitivity:**
- 500 paired null replicates per partition, seeded at **20261114** (discovery) and **20261115** (validation).
- Burn in **30×number_of_partition_cameras** pair proposals once.
- Between successive replicate matrices, make **15×number_of_partition_cameras** further pair proposals (including no-op proposals). Do not tune acceptance or iteration count by outcome; record attempts and nontrivial trades, and check exact margins before and after every 100 replicates.
- Each replicate's **single** ten-camera selection is the **same indexed selection** as the original empirical draw with that replicate number. Compute the **median of valid park-pair β_sim** in discovery (21 pairs) and validation (10 pairs), alongside β_sor and β_sne; keep empty park species sets unestimable rather than assigning 1.0 dissimilarity.
- Thus each paired replicate (`observed_j, null_j`) uses the identical camera opportunity/sample selection but different label allocations conditional on the fixed source margins. This is a **paired camera-subsampling and conditional-randomization sensitivity**, not a formal set of 500 independent reserve replicates. Finite-swap chain mixing/uniformity over all fixed-margin matrices is not proven.
- Record **all 31** within-partition eligible reserve-pair observed/null β_sim medians (21+10), each park's eligible camera count and row/column margin checks, as well as the 500 paired split-median contrasts. Raw camera names and camera×species matrices remain private to the job.

## Primary, secondary and stop conditions

Primary descriptive statistic per split: **`median_j[observed_βsim,j − null_βsim,j]`**, with fraction of the **500 paired draws** where observed is larger than the randomized matrix. A predeclared **strong observed-label sorting sensitivity** is reported only if (a) all 500 null and source draws are estimable in each partition, and (b) ≥475/500 paired draws have observed β_sim greater than null β_sim in **both** partitions. If positive in only one, report **split-dependent**, not general success. If none, report **no fixed-margins excess**. This is a **descriptive sensitivity decision, not a formal p-value**, because a single MCMC randomization chain with 500 correlated states and re-used cameras cannot establish type-I calibration.

Secondary: separate original/null partition medians of β_sne, β_sor; signed median difference, 5–95% descriptive range of paired differences (not CI), and fraction of null replicates with **β_sim > β_sne** (showing whether the original dominance headline remains trivially common even with preserved incidence margins). All park pairs and refusals are reported. No pairwise outcome selection.

Source integrity mismatch, missing two-partition support, any source SHA drift, nonpositive taxon-free groups, or any preservation failure → **technical stop**, no threshold rescue, taxon substitution, additional permutations or prospective ecological claims.

## Inference boundary

This test **does not preserve individual park-specific taxon incidence** — those affiliations are the target being randomized — but it does preserve each camera's detected richness and each taxon's full-partition camera occupancy. The resulting conditional null assumes **all within-partition cameras are exchangeable with respect to label assignment**. This is environmentally/geographically unrealistic where habitats, accessible source pools, animal movements, camera types or effort differ. Consequently, an excess may indicate **recorded park-specific sorting relative to that artificial exchangeability assumption**, not a biological mechanism or true occupancy excess. Effective detection still varies by species and site, and some park geography/labels may not match a single ecological community.

Do **not** call this independent field replication, species-level detectability correction, eSDM predictive improvement, significance from 500 artificial replicates, nonrandom competition, habitat filtering, isolation effects or measured ecosystem services. Original E4/E5/E7/E7-C/S14 outcomes remain **frozen**, with original E5 **0/17 qualified / G4 unpassed**.
