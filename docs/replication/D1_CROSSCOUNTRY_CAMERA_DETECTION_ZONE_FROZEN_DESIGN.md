## Target and strictly descriptive question

The observed response is authors' **effective detection distance (EDD)**, `full.r` (meters), derived from animal detections at camera traps. Does a response-blind **camera/trait/protocol** model trained in all other countries lower prediction error for published log EDD in a country never used for fitting, relative to a global mean and a camera-brand-only baseline? This is *observation-process variability*, **not** independent counts of camera misses/true animal passages. It cannot identify `p=P(register|independent passage)` or pass E5 G4 without additional linked passage/calibration data and crossed response protocols. It does not establish the true ecology of animal range or relative activity.

## Frozen extraction

One CSV row is one published species × effective detection zone; use `ID` as the unique source zone key, `ref` as paper/study metadata, `Country` as geographic fold. Require all headers `ID, ref, Country, full.r, ndet, ct_brand, body_mass, ct_height, T, data.order`; missing headers -> terminal `SCHEMA_STOP` without tuning. Require nonblank `ID`, and unique `ID` across the CSV or fail `SCHEMA_STOP`; no row-index pseudo-identity. Filter **Mammalia analogue orders only**: case-insensitive `Carnivora`, `Primates`, `Rodentia`, `Ungulata` (no Aves). Preserve all stages' exclusion counts. Complete-case and finite positivity rules: `full.r>0` meters, `ndet>=50` whole-number detection events, `body_mass>0`, `ct_height>0`, nonblank country/brand/order, and positive `T` numeric seconds or literal `contact`, which is set to 1500 seconds according to the authors' published metadata convention. Exclude invalid records; never impute, adjust cutoffs or select favorable countries/species.

Three models use the **identical eligible rows** and source-country holdout; fit each fold only on countries other than the heldout country:
- **M0 global:** mean training `log(full.r)` across all training zones.
- **M1 brand:** a smoothed brand mean with exactly **10** pseudo-zones toward M0; unseen brand falls back to M0.
- **M2 technical + traits:** unpenalized intercept and ridge **alpha=10** on standardized `log(body_mass)`, `log(ct_height)`, `log1p(T_seconds)` and one-hot `ct_brand`, `data.order`. Numeric means, standard deviations and category roster fit on training only; constant numeric features set to zero. Unseen holdout category maps to zeros. Predictions are log EDD; evaluate `abs(log(full.r)-pred)`; no clipping or after-response retraining. Ridge fitted with deterministic Gauss-Jordan solver and no external package required.

## Country holdout, support and decision

Evaluate **every country with >=2 eligible EDD zones** by leave-one-*country*-out; countries with 1 eligible zone are excluded from scoring but remain in other countries' training. Global support gate: >=40 eligible EDD zones, >=5 distinct countries overall, >=4 scored heldout countries (each >=2 zones), and >=30 training rows for every fold. Otherwise terminal `INSUFFICIENT_COUNTRY_SUPPORT`, never relax it after seeing the response. The rows sharing study or site are dependent; report median and mean per-country MAE, row-weighted pooled MAE, heldout zone and country support; **no p-value, CI or 167 independent country replicates**.

**Primary predeclared descriptive contrast:** M2 minus M0 improvement `MAE_M0 - MAE_M2` (in *absolute log-meter error*); a bounded useful-transfer label requires **>=+0.05** pooled log-error gain and **>=60%** scored countries with strictly positive country mean gain. M1 is a non-tuned secondary comparison; report M2−M1 and M1−M0 signed. Country imbalance and unseen camera-brand/trait distributions are noted, not tuned away. A positive result supports *partially transferable effective detection-zone geometry*, never the four-stage esdm observation parameter `p` or overall eSDM superiority; a negative result means this predeclared predictor failed, not that all detection calibration is impossible.
