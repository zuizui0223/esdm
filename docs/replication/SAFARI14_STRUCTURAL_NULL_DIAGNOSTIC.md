# S14 retrospective structural-null audit: when is turnover dominance informative?

**Interpretation audit, 2026-11-14.** Source: the completed, previously opened [Safari14 observed mammal assemblage result](SAFARI14_SPATIAL_BETA_FROZEN_RESULT_RECEIPT.json). **The outcome was already known** (500/500 camera resamples turnover-dominant in both park partitions) when this sensitivity was conceived. It is NOT an a priori confirmatory hypothesis test, a new wildlife-source opening, independent source/park replication, occupancy adjustment, ecological fit or formal null-model significance test.

## Why a counterexample is needed

The original documented finding is valid as an **observed-data description**: at fixed 10 cameras per eligible reserve, the median Simpson component of between-park detected mammal dissimilarity exceeded the nestedness-resultant component in both the fixed discovery seven-park and validation five-park partitions. But the statement `β_sim > β_sne` could also arise when species are simply **randomly assigned to parks** with no habitat partitioning or distinct underlying assembly process. Turnover/nestedness is a mathematical dissimilarity decomposition; dominance is not, in itself, proof that turnover is unusually large, environmentally mediated, or caused by dispersal limitation.

**Finite test from already frozen aggregates:** Is the criterion also satisfied in an explicitly artificial community that preserves the original per-park rarefied *species-count medians*, but otherwise draws independently from the same available set of 38 mammal labels? If so, the original 500/500 dominance criterion is a stable descriptive observation but **not an ecological null rejection**.

## Three deliberately simple counterfactual sampling scenarios

For **each** artificial iteration, draw one *independent* species set of its original median richness per park, **without replacement** from the same 38 labels, using exponential-race sampling. Simulate exactly **500** draws per scenario with documented seed `20261114` (separate deterministic streams for each scenario). The inclusion weight for mammal label `i` is `w_i = original_annotation_row_count_i ^ α`. Three post-outcome **illustrative sensitivities** are explored, not fitted or selected by predictive performance:

- **α = 0:** all 38 mammal labels equally available to every park (uniform artificial null); no park differentiation beyond park observed richness.
- **α = 0.5:** moderate influence of globally common labels.
- **α = 1:** strong preference for globally frequent image labels. This is intentionally **not** a realistic estimate of actual reserve-level encounter rate and must not be given an ecological interpretation by itself.

For each artificial draw, all **21 discovery park pairs** and **10 validation park pairs** are decomposed using the same pairwise Baselga `β_sor = β_sim + β_sne` formulas as the original, then the partition median is taken **separately for each component**. Report scenario median `β_sim` and `β_sne`, the fraction of artificial draws where median turnover exceeds median nestedness, and the original frozen observed medians alongside. All three α values must be reported, not only the one best matching observed results. Missing label counts or original source mismatch is a terminal input stop.

**Do not** use any resulting artificial draw frequency as a bootstrap CI, p-value, or likelihood of observed ecology. Original empirical 500 camera resamples reuse cameras; artificial 500 sets are generated by a different statistical mechanism and are not sampling-distribution equivalents. The nulls use only per-park *richness medians*, not observed source park-by-mammal identity, actual site/camera detection, species co-occurrence, geographic covariates or phylogeny.

## Interpretation boundaries

- If uniform random assignments also show turnover dominance, that falsifies the **claim that dominance alone demonstrates environmental filtering, conservation complementarity beyond chance or non-random species replacement**.
- If conclusions change between α=0 and α=1, their interpretation is sensitive to assumed species encounter frequency; no source-frequency-preserving null has yet been tested.
- If original observed turnover differs from the illustrative null medians, that is **descriptive scenario contrast only** because the artificial models omit actual source prevalence and site observational selection. No "positive non-random assembly" finding may be promoted.
- The original positive *observation* (turnover component exceeds nestedness across the eligible park partitions) remains true. The appropriate future test requires camera-by-species incidence data, operational effort/coverage, regional species pool definition and null models preserving the relevant marginals, plus independent parks/monitoring networks. Do **not** reopen the original S14 source or tune its earlier thresholds to recover a stronger claim.
